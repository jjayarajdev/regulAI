Step 1📄

The regulation as it lands today

TICO publishes the Texas Statistical Plan as a 89-page PDF. Every Texas residential insurer hand-codes ETL to translate it. When the regulation changes, that ETL is rewritten — months of work, compliance risk, and tribal knowledge.

Step 2🧠

The KG that read the regulation

Sentinel (LLM) reads the prose. A deterministic parser handles the tabular wire-format sections. Both feed a closed-vocabulary KG with citation-grounded provenance. Below: live data showing the legal values for column 5–6 of every Premium record TICO accepts. The result is the spec.
