A.34

Tenure-discount companion

Authority: 28 TAC §5.9970(c)(2)  ·  Bulletin: B-2026-Q3-094  ·  Effective: Jan 1, 2026
Regulator's text
"When code L appears in column 78 of the premium exposure record, the carrier shall place a companion code from the set {J, K, M} in column 79, within five characters of the L, identifying the qualifying tenure tier."
In plain language: L on its own isn't enough. It must always pair with J (3–5 yr), K (6–9 yr), or M (10+ yr). A missing companion means the regulator can't tell which tier of discount was applied — the filing fails this rule and the carrier risks a per-record penalty.
