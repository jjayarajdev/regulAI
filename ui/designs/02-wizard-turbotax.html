Which entity are you filing for, and where does it go?

Texas's 2026 statistical plan rules direct insurers to file with TDI's designated statistical agent — that's TICO. We'll fill in everything we can from your carrier record; tell us which entity is the filer and pick a destination.

⚠
What changed in 2026: Direct submission to TDI's portal was discontinued. All residential, private passenger auto, and commercial lines statistical plans must now route through TICO per 28 TAC §5.7008. The cadence for TPA is quarterly; residential moved to monthly effective April 1.
