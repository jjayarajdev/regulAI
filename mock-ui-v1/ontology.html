Coverage
A contractual undertaking by the Insurer to indemnify the Insured against loss arising from specified Perils, subject to stated Limits, Deductibles, and Conditions. A Coverage always belongs to exactly one Policy and covers one or more Perils; its effective period is strictly nested within its Policy.

01Properties

coverageId UUID Opaque unique identifier. Stable across source-system re-keys. required
type CoverageType Enumerated canonical type (CoverageA_Dwelling, CoverageC_Personal, Liability_E, …). required
limit Money Policy limit. Must be positive; currency-tagged; basis defaults to "Stated". required
deductible Money Retention applied before indemnity. May be zero for liability coverages. optional
effectivePeriod DateRange When coverage is in force. Must be subset of Policy.effectivePeriod. required
covers Set<Peril> Enumerated perils included. Initialized from CoverageType defaults at bind. required · ≥1
excludes Set<Peril> Perils explicitly excluded. Wins over covers by design. optional
endorsements List<Endorsement> Form additions modifying coverage scope (HO 15, HO 04 90, DP 17, …). optional
