This section provides additional information on some of the fields for reporting reason codes. 

## **Reason source indicator** 

When the insurer's decision to cancel, nonrenew, or decline relied in whole or in part on information sourced from aerial imagery or other third parties, note that as appropriate in the reason source indicator field. Insurance Code §551.002 requires that the insurer's written statement to applicants and policyholders must state the source of the information on which the insurer relied. 

## **Reason codes** 

See the record layout for the full list of reasons and their codes. The following provides additional information for some of the reasons. 

- 1) Increase in hazard—Use for a cancellation reason if the following happened: 

   - a) The policy was canceled due to an increase in hazard covered by the policy that was within the control of the insured and would have produced an increase in the premium rate of the policy. Insurance Code §551.104(c) 

   - b) The policy had been in effect less than 60 days and the insurer identifies a condition that: (A) creates an increased risk of hazard; (B) was not disclosed in the application for insurance coverage; and (C) is not the subject of a prior claim. Insurance Code §551.104(g)(1) 

- 2) Inspection report not accepted—Use for a cancellation reason if before the effective date of the policy, the insurer does not accept a copy of a required inspection report that was completed by an inspector and is dated within 90 days of the effective date of the policy. Insurance Code §551.104(g)(2) 

- 3) Exposure to loss – liability—Use if the reason for action is due to 1) the insured or applicant's personal liability risk under the liability sections of the policy or 2) the characteristics or activities of the insureds or applicants. Do not use to reflect first-party claims history. 

- 4) Exposure to loss – insurer's concentration of risk—Use if the insurer is not writing new business or renewing policies because of the insurer's excessive exposure in a particular area or subline. Do not use if the reason is due to wildfire or wind/hail/hurricane exposure. 

- 5) Insurer withdrawing from the market—Use if the insurer has filed a withdrawal plan with TDI that has been approved. Do not use any other reason codes in conjunction with this one.

- 6) Location of risk—Use if insurer does not write in certain areas and none of the "exposure to loss" reasons apply. 

- 7) Credit or insurance score—Use if the policy was nonrenewed or declined because of the insured's or applicant's credit score. Insurance Code §559.052(a)(2) prohibits insurers from denying, cancelling, or nonrenewing a residential property policy solely on the basis of credit information without considering other applicable underwriting factors independent of credit. If credit or insurance score is a reason, there must also be another reason. 

- 8) Condition of property—If the Action Type is due to the condition of the property, use all applicable reasons codes related to condition of the property. If the nonrenewal or declination is due to not writing particular types of roofs (for example, wood-shake roofs), then code this as "Other, insurer's action" as opposed to "Condition of property." 

- 9) Value of home—Use this code when the property exceeds the limit set in the insurer's underwriting guidelines. 

- 10) Insured's request—Use for a cancellation reason if: 

   - a) The insured's representative cancels the policy because of the insured's death. 

   - b) The insured no longer has an insurable interest in the property (for example, they sold it). 

   - c) For any other reason as long as the cancellation was at the insured's request. 

- 11) Other, insurer's action—Use if: 

   - a) The insured submitted a fraudulent claim. Insurance Code §551.104(b)(2) 

   - b) TDI determined that the continuation of a policy would violate the law. Insurance Code §551.104(b)(3) 

   - c) The insured failed to pay their membership dues required by the insurer as a condition for coverage. 

   - d) For any reason not otherwise listed.
