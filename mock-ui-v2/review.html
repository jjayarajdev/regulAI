SOP-COAST-03 · Named Storm vs. Windstorm A loss shall be coded WH when (a) the loss date-time falls within ±6 hours of named-storm landfall as defined by the most recent NWS advisory, and (b) the property lies within the storm's 34-knot wind radius at that time. A loss otherwise attributable to wind is coded WN. If adjuster notes affirmatively document a non-named wind source (e.g., convective cell, tornado spawned outside the named-storm envelope), the specialist may override to WN with written rationale.
