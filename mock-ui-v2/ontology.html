WH — Windstorm (Named Storm)
Cause-of-loss code assigned when loss is attributable to wind associated with an NWS-declared named storm (hurricane or tropical storm), distinguished from WN (windstorm, all other). Selection is governed by the storm's 34-knot wind radius at or within ±6 hours of landfall per SOP-COAST-03 §4.2. This distinction drives catastrophe reinsurance loss reporting, TICO aggregate accumulation statistics, and ISO loss development data — a WH→WN misclassification is retroactively correctable only through a formal TICO challenge process.
