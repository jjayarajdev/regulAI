# Commissioner's Bulletin B-2027-Q1-117

## Reinsurance Coverage Indicator on Premium Records

**Issued:** December 18, 2026
**Effective:** January 1, 2027
**Affects:** Texas Statistical Plan for Residential Risks — Section C (Premium Record Layout)
**Authority:** Texas Insurance Code §38.001(b); 28 TAC §5.9970

---

### §1. Purpose

To improve the Department's visibility into reinsurance coverage of Texas residential property risks, the Commissioner is amending Section C of the Statistical Plan to add a new mandatory data element: the *Reinsurance Coverage Indicator*.

### §2. New Required Field

Effective for all Premium records with accounting dates on or after January 1, 2027, every record submitted under Section C must include the following field:

**Reinsurance Coverage Indicator (RCI)** — column 184, length 1 character

This field replaces the existing reserved (SKIP) bytes at column 184 of the Premium record layout established in Section C of the 2026 Statistical Plan.

### §3. Code Values

The Reinsurance Coverage Indicator must contain exactly one of the following values:

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `Y`  | The risk is reinsured through the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association (TWIA) or another approved Texas reinsurance facility. |
| `N`  | The risk is NOT reinsured under any TWIA or Texas reinsurance arrangement. |
| `U`  | Reinsurance status is unknown at the time of the original Premium record submission, or is determined post-bind. Records coded `U` must be reconciled and resubmitted with `Y` or `N` within ninety (90) days of the original submission. |

### §4. No Backfill Required

Premium records submitted for accounting periods prior to January 1, 2027 are not affected by this Bulletin. No backfill is required for prior periods, and prior-period resubmissions may continue to use the existing layout.

### §5. Effect on Other Reports

This Bulletin amends Section C only. The Loss Record Layout (Section D), Notice Record Layout (Section E), and Notice Count Record Layout (Section G) are not affected.

### §6. Rationale

The Department's 2026 stress test of Texas residential catastrophe exposure revealed material gaps in the visibility of reinsurance coverage at the policy level, particularly for coastal and named-storm-prone territories. Adding the Reinsurance Coverage Indicator at the policy level allows TICO to compute aggregated reinsurance penetration by territory and by line of business, supporting the Commissioner's ongoing evaluation of TWIA depopulation and reinsurance market adequacy.

### §7. Reconciliation Requirement

Carriers electing to use the `U` code in the original submission must implement an internal reconciliation process. Records coded `U` for more than ninety (90) days without resubmission will be reported in the monthly TICO data quality report and may be referred to TDI for further inquiry under existing reasonable-care provisions of the Statistical Plan.
