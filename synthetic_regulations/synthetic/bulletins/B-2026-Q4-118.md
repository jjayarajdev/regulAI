# Commissioner's Bulletin # B-2026-Q4-118 (synthetic)

**TO:** All insurers writing residential property insurance in Texas

**RE:** Temporary modification of credit-score reporting requirement during state-declared catastrophe periods

**Date:** December 15, 2026 (synthetic — for RegulAI POC demonstration only; not an actual TDI bulletin)

---

## §1. Authority and effective date

Pursuant to the Commissioner's authority under Texas Insurance Code §38.001 and §551.001, and in coordination with the Texas Department of Public Safety regarding state-declared catastrophe responses, this bulletin temporarily modifies the reporting requirements established by Tex. Ins. Code §559.052(a)(2) with respect to credit-based insurance score citation in declination notices issued during state-declared catastrophe periods.

The modification described in this bulletin takes effect for declination notices with notification dates **on or after January 1, 2027**.

## §2. Modification of credit-score reporting constraint

Section E of the Texas Statistical Plan for Residential Risks, in conjunction with Tex. Ins. Code §559.052(a)(2), is hereby modified as follows:

**Reason Code L ("Credit or insurance score")** may be cited as a sole reason for declination of a residential property application when:

1. The application is for risk located within an area subject to a state-declared catastrophe order issued by the Texas Department of Public Safety within the preceding 180 days; and
2. The carrier's underwriting standards specify that adverse credit-based insurance scores constitute material underwriting risk during the recovery period;

In such cases, the requirement that Reason Code L be accompanied by at least one additional reason code is suspended.

## §3. Effect on TSPR Section E reporting

For purposes of Reason Code List validation:

**Code L:** the `companion_required` constraint is set to `FALSE` for all declination notices with notification dates on or after January 1, 2027.

All other validation rules for Reason Code List entries remain unchanged. Specifically, the `must_appear_alone` requirement for **Code J** ("Insurer withdrawing from the market") is unaffected by this bulletin and remains in effect.

## §4. Sunset

This modification expires December 31, 2027 unless extended by subsequent bulletin. Carriers shall resume the §559.052(a)(2) companion-code requirement for Reason Code L upon expiration unless otherwise directed.

## §5. Operational guidance

Carriers should update their statistical reporting systems and reference data to reflect the modification described in §2 above. The Texas Insurance Consulting Organization (TICO) will accept Section E records with Reason Code L as the sole reason on or after January 1, 2027 without rejection, provided all other Section E requirements are met.
