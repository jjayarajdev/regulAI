## RE: Hurricane Ian Data Call — Weekly Claims and Catastrophe Reporting

## Background

Hurricane Ian made landfall in Florida on September 28, 2022, as a Category 4 storm. The Office of Insurance Regulation (OIR) is invoking its authority under Sections 624.307 and 624.318, Florida Statutes, to collect timely and granular claims data from all authorized insurers transacting property and casualty insurance in Florida. This memorandum supersedes any prior catastrophe data call directives currently in effect with respect to claims arising from Hurricane Ian.

## Authority

This data call is issued pursuant to:

- Section 624.307, F.S. — General powers; duties of the office
- Section 624.318, F.S. — Examinations; access to records
- Section 627.351(6), F.S. — Citizens Property Insurance Corporation reporting requirements
- Section 627.062, F.S. — Rate standards (informing future rate filings)

## Reporting Requirements

### Scope

All property and casualty insurers transacting in Florida — including Citizens Property Insurance Corporation, surplus lines carriers, and licensed admitted carriers — shall report claims data arising from Hurricane Ian (Catastrophe Code IAN-2022) according to the schedule and format set forth below.

### Cadence

Reports shall be submitted to the OIR on a weekly basis, with the first report due no later than 5:00 PM Eastern Time on Wednesday, October 12, 2022, and continuing every Wednesday thereafter until the OIR releases insurers from the call. The OIR anticipates the call will remain in effect for not less than 24 months following the date of this memorandum.

### Required Data Elements

Each weekly submission shall include the following per-claim data fields:

1. **Claim Number** — Carrier's internal claim identifier
2. **Catastrophe Code** — IAN-2022 (mandatory for inclusion)
3. **Date of Loss** — In YYYY-MM-DD format
4. **Date Reported** — When the insured first notified the carrier
5. **County of Loss** — FIPS county code, per FL counties
6. **ZIP Code of Loss Location** — 5-digit, plus 4-digit extension if available
7. **Policy Form** — HO-3, HO-5, HO-6, DP-1, DP-3, or other (specify)
8. **Coverage A Limit** — Dwelling coverage amount on the affected policy
9. **Cause of Loss** — Wind, Storm Surge, Flood, Tree Fall, Power Surge, or Other
10. **Catastrophe Deductible Applied** — Dollar amount or percentage
11. **Reserve Amount** — Current claim reserve, including ALAE
12. **Paid to Date** — Cumulative paid including ALAE
13. **Claim Status** — Open, Closed-Paid, Closed-Without-Payment, or Reopened
14. **Litigation Indicator** — Y/N
15. **Assignment of Benefits Indicator** — Y/N
16. **Suit Filed Date** — If applicable, otherwise null

### Submission Format

Reports shall be filed via the OIR Catastrophe Reporting System (CRS) at https://apps.fldfs.com/CRS. Submissions shall use the CRS Fixed-Width ASCII Format Specification, Version 3.2, available on the OIR website. Each line shall be 240 characters fixed-width with the column-positioned fields described in the CRS Format Specification.

### Aggregate Summary Required

In addition to the per-claim detail, each insurer shall submit an aggregate weekly summary including:

- Total number of claims reported (by county)
- Total reserves outstanding (by line of business)
- Total payments to date (by line of business)
- Total number of closed claims (by status: paid vs. without payment)
- Average days from date of loss to first inspection

## Special Provisions for Citizens Property Insurance Corporation

Pursuant to Section 627.351(6)(h), F.S., Citizens shall report all data elements above plus the following additional fields for each affected policy:

- **Account Code** — Personal Lines, Commercial Lines, or Coastal Account
- **Replacement Cost Tier** — Below $700,000, $700,000–$1M, or above $1M
- **Assessment Recovery Status** — Indicator of whether the claim qualifies for emergency assessment recovery under the Coastal Account

## Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with this data call may result in administrative action under Section 624.307, F.S., including monetary penalties of up to $5,000 per business day per violation. Material misrepresentation in a data call submission shall be considered an unfair trade practice under Section 626.9541, F.S., and may result in license suspension or revocation.

## Effective Date

This Informational Memorandum is effective immediately upon issuance and remains in effect until revoked by subsequent OIR action. The first reporting cycle begins October 5, 2022 (date of issuance).
